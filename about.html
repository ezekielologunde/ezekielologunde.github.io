About

Who I am, what I focus on, and the work behind Cyntraix.

01

In brief

I am a cybersecurity professional, educator and researcher with a record of building rigorous, industry-aligned academic programs at community college, university and K–12 levels.

I am pursuing a Doctor of Engineering in Cybersecurity Analytics at The George Washington University, expected September 2027. My research examines AI-enabled security governance, adversarial machine learning defenses, explainable AI, and risk quantification in enterprise and educational environments. I chose an engineering doctorate because I want to build and solve real problems.

As founder of Cyntraix, I provide enterprise security assessments, Zero Trust architecture design and risk governance advisory to organizations working with NIST CSF, CMMC 2.0 and HITRUST. Before I recommend a tool, I run it in my own security lab.

I bring experience in curriculum governance, faculty collaboration, departmental planning and workforce development, and a commitment to academic excellence, student equity, and the meeting point of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity defense.
