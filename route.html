  18. N-18Pivot . path 6/7May 2026 onward

    Breakwater

    GWU, SEAS 8414, Analytical Tools for Cyber

    An end-to-end security-analytics pipeline for a simulated OT/IoT network: discovery, device identity, vulnerability triage, attack paths, a digital twin and post-quantum readiness. Phase 1 admitted 20 hosts, 77% of the declared 26, with 0 phantom hosts and 248 NVD CVEs matched. Later phases: a federated intrusion-detection model with poisoning and privacy defenses, and reinforcement-learning agents kept behind safety controllers. An ACM-format paper is in preparation. Simulated network only.
