03Chapter three

The craft: proof of work

I run what I recommend, measure it, and write down the trade-offs.

A · Security monitoring lab

Two small machines, one log pipeline

Before I recommend SIEM, host-based detection or monitoring tooling to Cyntraix clients, I run it at home: two Proxmox VE nodes, a firewall VM, a DNS sinkhole and a reverse proxy, all forwarding logs over syslog into one SIEM index, plus a host-based intrusion detection manager with agents on the Linux hosts. Scheduled searches produce a daily digest and a critical-event watch under a search-only service account.

~304,700events in 24 hours from five log sources
~3,700host-based detection alerts over 7 days

B · Breakwater, doctoral coursework

Evidence-bounded vulnerability intelligence for OT/IoT

In George Washington University coursework (SEAS 8414, Analytical Tools for Cyber) I built an end-to-end security-analytics pipeline against a containerized OT/IoT simulation network, one phase at a time: asset discovery, device identity, vulnerability triage, attack paths mapped to MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, a digital twin for testing remediation, and post-quantum readiness. An ACM-format paper is in preparation.

All Breakwater devices are simulated containers. No real operational network was scanned.
